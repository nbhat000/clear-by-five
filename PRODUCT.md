## Product Purpose

Clear by Five is an AI operations consultancy. It identifies costly, repetitive workflows (missed-lead follow-up, scheduling, dispatch, customer service, reporting), then designs and implements practical automations that work with the company's existing systems. Success is fewer hours of administrative work, faster response times, and measurable financial impact.

## Positioning

Operational and ROI-first, not "AI transformation." Founder-led: the person who diagnoses the workflow builds the fix. Starts with a paid, fully credited 7-day audit that must find credible annual value or the fee is refunded. Works inside the tools the company already runs.

## Operating Context

Engagement path: 20-minute opportunity scan (free call) → 7-day workflow audit ($2,500 founding-partner price, credited toward implementation, refunded if less than $25,000 in credible annual value is found) → ~30-day implementation → ongoing improvement as needed.
